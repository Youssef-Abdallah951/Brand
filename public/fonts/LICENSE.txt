Norican — brand wordmark font (self-hosted, latin subset)
=========================================================
Source: Google Fonts — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Norican
Designer: Vernon Adams
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt at the source repo)
File: norican-latin.woff2 (latin subset, OFL-licensed, woff2)

Chosen to visually match the script lettering of the official "Jia"
logo artwork (flowing signature script, capital-J loop with an
underlining swash). Used ONLY for the brand wordmark.
